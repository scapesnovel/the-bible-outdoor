How it works

  • On publish, the bot renders your Short — voice-over, music, cinematic background — and premieres it at your time.
  • 1 custom that day → bot posts 1 of its own. 2 customs → bot skips the day. Its verses simply move to future days.
  • You may reuse any verse — your explanation makes it unique. The bot may also revisit your verses later with its own reflection.
  • Cancel anytime from the queue — already-uploaded videos are deleted from YouTube automatically.
