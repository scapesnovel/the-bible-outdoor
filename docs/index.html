ℹ️ How it works

• When you publish, the bot renders your Short (voice-over, music, background) and schedules it at your chosen time.

• 1 custom that day → the bot posts only 1 of its own. 2 customs → the bot skips the day. Its verses aren't lost — they move to future days automatically.

• You may reuse any verse (even ones the bot used) — your explanation makes it unique. The bot may also reuse your custom verses later with its own reflection.

• Cancel anytime from the queue — if already uploaded, it's deleted from YouTube.
